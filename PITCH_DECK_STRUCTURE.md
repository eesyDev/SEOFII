## Slide 1: Hook (7 seconds)

## Slide 3: The Solution (15 seconds)

## Design Tips

1. **Dark mode** — matches your product aesthetic (black + #fd356e pink accent)
2. **No charts with 10 lines** — one metric per slide max
3. **Screenshots > descriptions** — show the product, don't talk about it
4. **Numbers > words** — "98.6% margin" beats "very profitable"
5. **One idea per slide** — if you need 2 bullets, make 2 slides

|-------|------|
