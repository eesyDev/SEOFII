# SEOBrief — Live Demo Script for Investors

> **Duration:** 3–4 minutes  
> **Goal:** Show, don't tell. Let investors feel the "magic moment" — URL to action plan in 2 minutes.

---

## Pre-Demo Setup

### 1. Choose a Demo URL
Pick a real business website that investors will recognize or relate to. Good options:
- A portfolio company of the investor (if public)
- A well-known SMB in the target region
- A competitor of a portfolio company
- Example: `https://casper.com/mattresses` (e-commerce) or `https://hubspot.com/blog` (content)

**Pro tip:** Test the URL beforehand. Make sure it generates a good report in under 90 seconds.

### 2. Prepare Two Browsers
- **Browser 1:** Your app, logged in as demo user
- **Browser 2:** Backup — already-loaded report page (in case live generation fails)

### 3. Have These Ready
- Landing page open in fullscreen
- Your phone as timer (to show "2 minutes" claim)
- Report page ready to scroll through

---

## Demo Script

### Phase 1: The Hook (20 seconds)

**You say:**
> "Let me show you why 73% of small business owners think SEO is 'too complicated.' Then I'll show you how we fix it."

**Action:** Open Ahrefs or Semrush in a tab (if available) or show a screenshot.

**You say:**
> "This is what an SEO audit looks like today. 50 metrics, 12 dashboards, $500 from an agency, delivered in a week. The business owner has no idea what to actually DO."

**Action:** Switch to SEOBrief landing page.

---

### Phase 2: The Input (15 seconds)

**You say:**
> "Here's what we built. I'm going to analyze a real website — [name the URL you picked]. Watch this."

**Action:** Paste the URL into the input field on the landing page.

**You say:**
> "I just paste the URL. No login required for the first report."

**Action:** Click "Analyze my site". Start your phone timer.

---

### Phase 3: The Processing (30 seconds — talk over it)

**While the spinner runs, you say:**
> "Right now, behind the scenes, we're doing four things in parallel:
> 1. Pulling the top 10 Google results for this page via DataForSEO
> 2. Scraping every competitor — their headings, blocks, word count, schema markup
> 3. Running Claude AI to compare and generate recommendations
> 4. Checking PageSpeed scores
> 
> This used to take an SEO agency 3 days. We do it in about 90 seconds."

**Action:** Keep eye contact. Don't stare at the screen. The processing is your "mic drop" moment.

---

### Phase 4: The Reveal — Report Dashboard (90 seconds)

**When report loads:**

**You say:**
> "Done. [Check timer] — 1 minute 47 seconds. Now let me show you what the business owner gets."

#### 4A: Summary Cards (15 seconds)

**Scroll to:** Top of report — summary cards section.

**You say:**
> "First, the summary. Word count gap — competitor averages 2400 words, this page has 820. Speed score — 72 vs competitor average of 89. And here are quick-win keywords they already rank for but could improve."

**Point at:** Each card as you mention it.

---

#### 4B: Quick Fixes (20 seconds)

**Scroll to:** Quick Fixes section.

**You say:**
> "This is the money shot. Not 'improve your SEO' — concrete tasks. 
> 
> First: change the title to this exact text [point at generated title]. 5 minutes, copy-paste.
> 
> Second: add this FAQ block [point at FAQ]. 30 minutes.
> 
> Third: add schema markup [point at JSON-LD]. Also 30 minutes.
> 
> A business owner can do all three this afternoon."

---

#### 4C: Competitor Comparison (20 seconds)

**Scroll to:** Competitor Comparison section.

**You say:**
> "For marketers who want depth — we show exactly why each competitor ranks higher.
> 
> Competitor #1: 2400 words vs 820. Has FAQ block. Has Product schema.
> Competitor #2: 6 H2/H3 headings vs their 3. Uses video block.
> 
> Every claim is backed by real scraped data, not AI hallucination."

---

#### 4D: Block Matrix (15 seconds)

**Scroll to:** Block Matrix.

**You say:**
> "This is my favorite. Visual matrix: which content blocks do you have vs. your competitors.
> 
> Red row: FAQ block — 4 out of 5 competitors have it, you don't. Priority: must.
> Yellow row: Video — 2 have it. Consider adding.
> 
> It's like a feature comparison table, but for your webpage."

---

#### 4E: Ready Content (20 seconds)

**Scroll to:** Ready Content section.

**You say:**
> "And for users who don't want to think — we literally write the content.
> 
> Title: [read it aloud]
> H1: [read it]
> Meta description: [read it]
> Intro paragraph: [read first sentence]
> FAQ with 6 questions and answers.
> Plus schema.org markup — copy, paste into HTML, done.
> 
> This alone saves 2–3 hours of copywriting."

---

### Phase 5: The Monitoring (15 seconds)

**Scroll to:** Monitoring section (or mention it if not visible).

**You say:**
> "And it doesn't stop here. We monitor this page every week. If a competitor adds a new block — you get an alert. If your ranking moves after you make changes — we track the correlation.
> 
> Over time, we build a database of what actually works in each niche."

---

### Phase 6: The Close (10 seconds)

**You say:**
> "So: paste URL, 2 minutes, concrete tasks, ready content, weekly monitoring.
> 
> Used to be $500 and 3 days from an agency. Now it's $25 a month and 2 minutes.
> 
> Any questions?"

**Action:** Stop sharing screen. Look at investors. Silence is fine — let them process.

## Common Investor Questions & Answers

### "How is this different from ChatGPT + Semrush?"
> "ChatGPT doesn't have live competitor data — it hallucinates. We scrape real top-10 pages, analyze their actual structure, and generate recommendations based on verified data. Plus, we do it in one click instead of 10 manual steps."

### "What's your moat?"
> "Three things:
> 1. Niche knowledge base — we accumulate patterns per industry. After 1000 pages in 'e-commerce mattresses,' we know exactly what blocks drive rankings.
> 2. Outcome data — we correlate page changes with actual ranking movements via Google Search Console. No one else does this at this price point.
> 3. Speed — 2 minutes end-to-end."

### "What if OpenAI builds this?"
> "OpenAI builds general-purpose tools. We're specialized: SEO data pipelines, niche-specific prompting, monitoring infrastructure. By the time they notice this niche, we'll have 10,000 pages of outcome data they can't replicate."

### "What's your CAC?"
> "We haven't scaled paid acquisition yet — this is pre-launch. Our model says <$15 CAC via Google Ads targeting 'SEO audit' keywords. At $25/mo Starter with 5% monthly churn, LTV is $150+. That's a 10:1 LTV/CAC ratio."

### "How much does each report cost you?"
> "About 7 cents. $0.03 for SERP data, $0.02 for Claude, $0.015 for supporting AI calls. We charge $5 per report on Pro plan. 98.6% gross margin."

### "Who's your ideal customer?"
> "Three segments:
> 1. SMB owners who know they need SEO but can't afford agencies
> 2. Freelance marketers who need to deliver audits fast
> 3. Small SEO agencies who want to white-label our engine
> 
> Our beachhead is EN and RU-speaking markets — underserved by US-centric tools."

### "What do you need the money for?"
> "Two things: growth marketing to hit $30K MRR, and product expansion — white-label, API, team workspaces. The engine works. Now we need users."

## Demo Disasters — How to Recover

| Problem | Recovery |
|---------|----------|
| Report takes >3 min | "Sometimes the AI is thinking extra hard — let me show you a completed report" [switch to backup browser] |
| Report fails | "This is beta — happens 1 in 20 times. Here's a report I generated this morning for the same URL" |
| Investor asks deep technical question | "Great question — [name], our CTO, can dive into the architecture. At a high level..." |
| Investor seems bored | Speed up. Skip to Quick Fixes + Ready Content. That's the wow moment. |
| "I don't understand SEO" | Perfect. Say: "Exactly. That's why we built this. You don't need to understand SEO to use it." |

## Post-Demo Checklist

- [ ] Did you show the product? (Not just talk about it)
- [ ] Did you mention the price? ($25/mo — makes it tangible)
